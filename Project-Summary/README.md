# ApexBridge Technologies : TPRM Assessment

> A simulated third-party risk management engagement, built as a practical GRC portfolio project.

## Purpose

This project works through a complete third-party risk management cycle for a fictional organisation  from establishing governance to reporting residual risk to management.

It exists to demonstrate practical GRC reasoning rather than document production.

## Simulated Organisation

**ApexBridge Technologies**  a fictional B2B SaaS and digital services company with approximately 300 employees, a cloud-heavy environment, and 47 third parties in scope.

All vendors, evidence, findings, incidents, approvals, and metrics are invented for this project.

## Role

**Junior GRC Analyst** supporting the TPRM programme.

Throughout the engagement, the analyst assesses, documents, tracks, and recommends.

Risk acceptance and strategic decisions rest with named risk owners and management.

## Lifecycle

**Govern → Inventory → Tier → Assess → Test → Remediate → Monitor → Report → Reassess on Change**

## Scope

- 47 third parties inventoried and tiered
- 6 vendors assessed in depth based on tier, data sensitivity, access, and criticality
- 11 evidence items reviewed
- 10 vendors under active monitoring

## Key Findings

Five findings and one observation were identified.

Notable examples include:

- A genuine and current SOC 2 report that was scoped to infrastructure rather than the access controls being assessed
- Privileged production access with no evidenced credential management
- A certificate that had been renewed on time but was never re-collected
- An unassessed subprocessor involved in a payment flow

## Risk Treatment Approach

Treatment varied by risk rather than defaulting to remediation.

Approaches included:

- Mitigation with a compensating control
- Mitigation plus contractual transfer
- Conditional acceptance with expiry
- Internal process correction

One proposed acceptance was refused because a proportionate mitigation existed.

No finding was closed solely on a statement that remediation was complete.

## Monitoring Approach

The monitoring model includes:

- Four cadence levels based on vendor tier
- A 12-event trigger matrix
- 10 defined metrics with thresholds and management actions

The underlying principle is:

> **Monitoring detects change; reassessment re-evaluates risk when it does.**

## What This Project Demonstrates

- Risk-based proportionality
- Evidence evaluation based on scope and currency rather than presence alone
- Distinguishing gaps from findings
- Treatment decisions with named ownership
- Proportional escalation
- Honest documentation of uncertainty
- Translating operational detail into management information

## Limitations

The project intentionally documents its limitations rather than presenting the simulated programme as fully mature.

- Assessment coverage reached 66% of the population in one cycle
- Two Tier 1 vendors lack current independent assurance
- One data-bearing vendor remains untiered
- No trend data exists
- Monitoring relies substantially on vendor self-notification

## Simulated Data Statement

**ApexBridge Technologies does not exist.**

All organisations, vendors, evidence documents, certificates, incidents, findings, approvals, and metrics in this repository are fictional and created for portfolio purposes.

This project does not represent work performed for any employer or client.
